Direction: Bazaar Signboard

The paint shop’s own signboard, turned into a website.

Hand-painted shop signs from Asan and Indra Chowk give the site its voice: bold condensed letters, enamel red and blue, pinstripe frames, Nepali beside English. Underneath, the tools sit on plain lime-wash white so paint colours read true.

  1. 01 Signboards frame, paint fills. Red and blue appear only on signs: the header, section titles, share images. Swatches, the house and the calculator sit on neutral ground.
  2. 02 Bilingual like the bazaar. The wordmark carries पाथीभरा पेन्ट्स above the English name, as shop signs do. All site copy stays English.
  3. 03 Numbers painted big. Litres, sq ft and rupees are set in signboard figures, so an estimate reads like a price board.
