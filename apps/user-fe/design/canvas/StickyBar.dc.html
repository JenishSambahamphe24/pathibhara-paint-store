Sticky contact bar
  • Where: every mobile page below 768 px, except the visualizer (its sheet already has “Send to store”) and the share sheet.
  • Order: Call · WhatsApp (widest, green) · Map. 48 px buttons, safe-area padding at the bottom.
  • Never covers content: pages add bottom padding equal to the bar’s height.
  • Never covers inputs: hides while a field is focused (keyboard open) and comes back on blur.
  • Calculator: the bar is replaced by the “Total so far · See estimate” bar on that page.
  • Tablet and desktop: no bar; WhatsApp sits in the header and contact details in the footer and Visit page.
  • WhatsApp messages are pre-written: page name plus shade codes or the estimate summary, so the store knows what the visitor means.
